## Audited but not promoted

- Mike Alger's face-blendshape male was rejected from runtime use at roughly one million triangles. It remains a source/reference candidate only.
- The uploaded female character GLB is static: no armature, skin, or animation clips were present. It must be rigged and deformation-tested in Blender before runtime promotion. Its embedded metadata identifies Braingapps, the [source model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/female-character-base-rigged-9245f75f5d0d45cb9e867d5133d5fdc1), and CC BY 4.0.

Names such as “Carter,” “Shvan,” “Saint 9mm,” “Marlow pump,” and “Mercer carbine” are internal fictional presentation names; source credits above remain unchanged.
